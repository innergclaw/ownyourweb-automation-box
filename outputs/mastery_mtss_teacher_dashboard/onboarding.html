Quick walkthrough

From sign-in to the MTSS workspace

This process is the same for teachers and administrators. Use the Microsoft account your school expects you to use for staff systems.

1

Open the staff link

Use this onboarding page from any computer or phone with a modern browser.

2

Choose Microsoft

Select Continue with Microsoft, then enter your school or professional Microsoft account.

3

Review first-time consent

Microsoft may ask to share your basic profile and email. These details identify your dashboard account.

4

Enter the workspace

After authentication, the MTSS dashboard opens with student review, intervention, GPA, assessment, and messaging views.

Testing another account

Use Restart sign-in test to clear the dashboard session and return to this page before choosing another Microsoft account.

Approved staff data access

Real student uploads are limited to approved Mastery staff accounts. Follow school data-governance requirements and use only authorized files.

Already authenticated? Open the dashboard. A teacher or administrator who cannot sign in should confirm the Microsoft email being used with the dashboard owner.
